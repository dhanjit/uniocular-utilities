# Protective eyewear counseling for patients with monocular vision (Boss et al., 2015)

**Citation:** Boss JD, Shah CT, Elner VM, Hassan AS. "Assessment of
Office-Based Practice Patterns on Protective Eyewear Counseling for Patients
With Monocular Vision." *Ophthalmic Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*.
2015;31(5):361–363.
**Identifiers:** PMID 25393903; DOI
[10.1097/IOP.0000000000000348](https://doi.org/10.1097/IOP.0000000000000348).
**Access:** Paywalled; a copy is hosted in the AOA evidence library
(<https://www.aoa.org/assets/documents/ebo/adult%20eye/415.02boss.pdf>),
unavailable (HTTP 503) on 2026-11-12.
**Verification:** Survey results verified via the PubMed abstract
(2026-11-12). The authors' numbered **counseling recommendations** (below) are
**from search digest** — taken from earlier notes that read the AOA-hosted
PDF; they could not be re-checked and are not in the abstract.

## Scope

Cross-sectional survey at an oculoplastic clinic in Michigan. 60 adults
(mean age 62.2, range 23–90) with vision of 20/400 or worse in one eye were
asked whether they recalled being counseled about protective eyewear.

## Key points

Verified (abstract):

- **56.7%** (34/60) did not recall education about protecting the better
  eye; **35.0%** (21/60) did; **8.3%** were unsure.
- Recall did not differ between people whose monocular vision came from
  trauma and those with other causes (p = 0.74).
- The authors call for protective-eyewear counseling to be built into the
  preferred practice pattern for monocular patients.

From search digest (not re-verified):

- Recommendations reportedly include: counsel all patients to protect the
  better eye with protective eyewear "at all awake hours"; use
  "polycarbonate or Trivex" lenses if possible; keep a spare pair; if those
  materials are unavailable, wear sturdy framed glasses with plastic lenses
  during waking hours; ANSI Z87.1 eyewear (with side shields if needed) for
  work or environmental exposure.

## Learnings for this project

- Independent confirmation of the counseling gap documented in
  [neimkin-custer-2017-eyewear-compliance](../neimkin-custer-2017-eyewear-compliance/).
- If its "all awake hours" and "polycarbonate or Trivex" wording is confirmed,
  it is one of the few peer-reviewed sources naming Trivex alongside
  polycarbonate for monocular adults.
