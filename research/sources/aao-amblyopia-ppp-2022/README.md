## Scope

US clinical guideline for diagnosing and treating amblyopia, mainly in
children. Includes a paragraph on protecting the better eye.

## Key points

- Risk: "the risk of vision loss in the better eye due to disease or injury
  has been estimated to be approximately 1:1000."
- Children: "amblyopic children who have vision of 20/50 or worse need to
  wear proper protective eyewear **full time**, even if they do not benefit
  from optical correction. A frame approved by the American National
  Standards Institute Standard No. Z87.1 with impact-resistant lenses (ASTM
  F803) should be worn daily and for low-eye-risk sports."
- Sport: impact-resistant goggles for most ball and contact sports;
  integrated head and face protection for higher-risk activities.
- General: "Functionally monocular patients should use approved protective
  eyewear when participating in contact sports or other potentially harmful
  activities, such as those that involve balls, pellet guns, paintballs, and
  personal use of fireworks. Special goggles, industrial safety glasses, side
  shields, and full-face shields should be used in these cases."

## Learnings for this project

- The "full time" wording is written for children; for functionally
  monocular people generally, this PPP speaks to **added** protection for
  risky activities. Quote it with that distinction.
- Specifies a Z87.1 frame, which is a stronger standard than dress frames.
