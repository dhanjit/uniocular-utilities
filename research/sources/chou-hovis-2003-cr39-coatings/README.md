# Durability of coated CR-39 industrial lenses (Chou & Hovis, 2003)

**Citation:** Chou BR, Hovis JK. "Durability of coated CR-39 industrial
lenses." *Optometry and Vision Science*. 2003;80(10):703–707.
**Identifiers:** PMID 14560121; DOI
[10.1097/00006324-200310000-00012](https://doi.org/10.1097/00006324-200310000-00012).
**Access:** Paywalled; abstract on PubMed.
**Verification:** Verified — findings below checked against the PubMed
abstract (2026-11-12).

## Scope

Twelve groups of CR-39 industrial plano lenses with various SR or SR+AR
coatings, in metal industrial frames. Ballistic impact by the Canadian
Standards Association protocol (ZEST staircase); abrasion by falling sand.

## Key points

- Uncoated lenses had the best impact resistance and worst abrasion
  resistance.
- SR coats caused mild to moderate reductions in impact resistance.
- SR+AR coats gave very good abrasion resistance but **severely reduced
  impact resistance**: such lenses "are too fragile to be used in industrial
  spectacles".
- Most SR-coated CR-39 lenses would likely meet the industrial high-velocity
  requirement.

## Learnings for this project

- AR coating on a brittle material compounds the problem; another reason to
  avoid CR-39 for a one-eyed wearer's everyday pair.
