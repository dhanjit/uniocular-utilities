# Lens impact resistance to airgun and rimfire projectiles (Rychwalski et al., 2003)

**Citation:** Rychwalski PJ, Packwood EA, Cruz OA, Holds JB. "Impact
resistance of common spectacle and safety lenses to airgun and rimfire
projectiles." *Journal of AAPOS*. 2003;7(4):268–273.
**Identifiers:** PMID 12917614; DOI
[10.1016/s1091-8531(03)00110-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1091-8531(03)00110-1).
**Access:** Paywalled; abstract on PubMed.
**Verification:** Verified — figures below checked against the PubMed
abstract (2026-11-12).

## Scope

Laboratory penetration test of glass, high-index and polycarbonate spectacle
and safety lenses, fired at with BBs, pellets and .22-calibre projectiles,
measuring projectile mass, velocity and energy and lens CT and power.

## Key points

- Maximum velocities: BB 221 m/s; pellet 210 m/s; CB.22 rimfire 204 m/s;
  .22 rimfire 290 m/s.
- BB penetration thresholds: **glass 84.0 m/s (2.4 J)**; **high-index
  107.7 m/s (4.09 J)**.
- **Polycarbonate spectacle and safety lenses were not penetrated by BBs at
  any velocity**; they were penetrated by CB.22 rimfire (**89.2 J**).
- The authors conclude polycarbonate offers "vastly superior" penetration
  resistance.

## Learnings for this project

- The clearest verified number for how far high-index plastic falls short of
  polycarbonate: polycarbonate survived BBs at more than twice the speed
  (roughly four times the energy) that penetrated high-index lenses.
- The high-index material's exact index is not given in the abstract.
